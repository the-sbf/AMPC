The Hard Conversation

There's a conversation you've been avoiding. You know the one. It's been sitting there for weeks.

This module teaches you how to have it — clearly, kindly, and without destroying the relationship.

≈ 5 minutes · interactive
Leadership Check-In

Before we teach you anything — let's see what you'd actually do.

You're the WHS coordinator at a mid-sized beef plant. Over the past two weeks, you've watched a shift supervisor — well-liked, been here 12 years — consistently skip the pre-start safety check on the boning line.

Other workers have started copying him. You know you need to say something. But he's respected. He's been here longer than you. And the last person who challenged him got the cold shoulder for weeks.

It's Tuesday morning. You see him skip the check again.

Leadership Check-In
Tuesday morning · Boning room floor

He's just skipped the check again. The team is watching. What do you do?

Be honest — pick what you'd actually do, not what you think you should do.

Every option either avoided the conversation — or had it badly.

Avoiding it lets the risk grow. Hinting wastes your authority. Blurting destroys the relationship. Documenting turns a conversation into a case.

There's a better way. It's called OBAN.

Module 3 Complete

You now have a framework for saying the hard thing well.

The skill isn't in knowing OBAN. It's in using it when your heart rate is up and someone's looking at you waiting for an answer.

That's what the plant task is for. One real conversation before the next session.
